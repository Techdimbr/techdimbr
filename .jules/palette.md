## 2026-10-09 - Required Indicators in Forms
**Learning:** Adding clear visual indicators (like an asterisk) to required form fields, along with explicit `aria-required="true"` attributes, significantly improves both cognitive clarity for general users and semantic understanding for screen reader users. It's an essential micro-interaction for forms.
**Action:** Always add visual indicators to required fields and accompany them with `aria-required="true"` on the input elements to ensure accessibility and better UX.
